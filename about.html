WHAT KEEPS ME CURIOUS

People are the point.

My motivation comes from the realities of neurodegenerative disease and barriers to care. I want to help develop neurotechnology that restores lost function and is robust, scalable, and affordable.

I’m drawn to the connections between bioelectronics, signal processing, and medical imaging—and to the challenge of turning research into something that can reach people beyond the lab.
